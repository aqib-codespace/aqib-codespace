### About me

My work sits between where your leads come from and where your sales happen. I build the web and mobile apps your business runs on, connect your tools, move data cleanly between them, and add alerts so you know the moment something breaks.

- **Full-stack apps:** SaaS products, dashboards and e-commerce with the MERN stack, Next.js and Supabase, plus mobile apps with React Native.
- **Automation:** 200+ automations built across GoHighLevel, n8n, Make and Zapier, with Node.js underneath when no-code stops being enough.
- **AI:** voice agents that answer inbound calls and make follow-up calls, and AI replies that start a conversation with a lead in seconds instead of days.

I use no-code when it's faster and write code when the no-code stops working. Knowing which one to use is most of the job.

### Selected client work

Most of my client work is private, so these are summaries rather than code.

- **Lead pipeline for a commercial real estate business:** three data sources feeding GoHighLevel, Notion and Slack. Around 6,600 records, no duplicates, and a daily self-check. Running in production for months.
- **AI sales pipeline:** Node.js cron jobs sync new leads to a database and GoHighLevel, OpenAI sends instant SMS and email replies, Vapi voice agents handle first calls and qualification, and the team gets alerts on high-priority deals. Turnaround time dropped by over 90%.
- **Lead qualification on autopilot:** an AI agent qualifies incoming leads against custom rules, so only qualified leads reach the admin, with automatic reminders that cut appointment no-shows.
